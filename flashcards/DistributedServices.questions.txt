Distributed Services: Questions

Focus on architecture trade-offs, service boundaries, data ownership, workflow reliability, and operations in the Simplifyme context.
🌍 From Monolith to Distributed Services
1. Why Distribute?

Which concrete signals indicate that a modular monolith should be split into distributed services, and which problems are usually not solved by splitting?
2. Spectrum Trade-offs

Compare Monolith, Modular Monolith, SOA, and Microservices. Where does operational complexity rise the fastest, and why?
3. Distributed Fallacies in Practice

Pick three fallacies of distributed computing and explain how each one can break a real workflow (for example subscription or payment expiration).
4. Bounded Context + Runtime

The lecture defines microservices as “Bounded Context + Distributed Runtime.” Why is this more precise than saying “small services”?
🧱 Boundaries, Granularity, and Data
5. Goldilocks Granularity

What are the symptoms of services that are too small vs too large, and how does workflow alignment help find the “just right” service boundary?
6. Distributed Monolith Smells

What evidence would show that a system is a distributed monolith rather than autonomous microservices?
7. Database-per-Service Rule

Why is a shared integration database considered a trap in microservices, and what specific coupling does it introduce between teams?
🔄 Workflows, Contracts, and Reliability
8. Sagas and Transaction Boundaries

Why do cross-service workflows use sagas instead of ACID transactions, and what is the role of compensating actions?
9. Contract Taxonomy

Explain the difference between domain events and integration events. Why should only integration events cross service boundaries?
10. Contract Evolution

Why are contracts “build inputs, not documentation”? Explain how expand-and-contract versioning reduces breaking changes.
11. Async Reliability Chain

How do Outbox, relay worker, Inbox deduplication, and DLQ fit together to improve reliability in at-least-once messaging?
